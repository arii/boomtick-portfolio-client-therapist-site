Notice of Right to a Good Faith Estimate

Last updated: October 2026

Under the federal No Surprises Act, healthcare providers (including psychotherapists) are required to provide clients who are uninsured or self-pay (not using insurance) with an estimate of expected charges for medical and mental health services.

Your Rights Under the No Surprises Act:

  • You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate (GFE) explaining how much your overall medical and mental health care will cost.
  • You have the right to receive this GFE in writing at least 1 business day before your scheduled clinical service or appointment. You can also request a GFE before scheduling a session.
  • If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you have the right to dispute the bill under federal guidelines.
  • Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate for your personal records.

Our Standard Fee Structure

In the spirit of complete financial transparency and simplicity, all psychotherapy services are billed at flat rates with no hidden fees or surge pricing:

  • Initial 30-Minute Consultation Call: Free ($0)
  • Individual Psychotherapy Session: $80 (per 50-minute clinical session)
  • Couples & Relational Therapy Session: $80 (per 50-minute clinical session)

Your expected total annual cost depends solely on your individual treatment goals, clinical progress, and frequency of sessions, which we will collaboratively evaluate together.

Federal Inquiries or Disputes

For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call the federal No Surprises Help Desk at 1-800-985-3059.
