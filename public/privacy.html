Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Marcella Mission Therapy, your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal information are of the utmost importance. This Privacy Policy outlines how personal data collected via our website form or contact coordinates is handled under California and federal laws (including CalOPPA).

Critical Confidentiality Notice: The contact inquiry form on this website is intended solely for initial scheduling and logistical coordination. Standard web forms and email are not secure, encrypted medical portals. Please do not submit highly sensitive, private medical or psychiatric histories through this form. Private Protected Health Information (PHI) should be saved for our live consultation.

1. Information We Collect

If you use the consultation inquiry form on our website, we collect the following basic contact details:

  • First and Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Preferred Session Format
  • Inquiry notes regarding scheduling or logistical preferences
  • Your communication consent preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your request for a free 30-minute consultation.
  • Coordinate appointment times and logistical formats.
  • Verify clinical alignment and fit for our practice.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your contact details or inquiry information with third-party advertisers or external marketers.

3. Confidentiality and HIPAA Compliance

While standard web inquiry forms do not constitute a formal clinical relationship or secure medical record, all messages are treated with strict professional confidentiality. If you decide to initiate therapy, your clinical records will be stored and transmitted using secure, fully HIPAA-compliant electronic health record (EHR) platforms directly governed by professional codes of ethics and California licensing regulations.

4. Cookie Consent

We use essential cookies strictly to support core site features, such as our integrated Cal.com scheduling modal. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, tracking pixels, or targeted profiling cookies.
