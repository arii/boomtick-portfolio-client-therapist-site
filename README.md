# Marcella Mission Therapy Website

Professional, BBS-compliant therapy practice website for **Marcella Mission, Pre-Licensed Professional** (Marriage & Family Therapist Trainee & Professional Clinical Counselor Trainee at Church Street Integral Counseling Center, supervised by **Dr. Derek Pehle, PsyD, CA License #21361**).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **California BBS Ethical Compliance:** Prominently features legal clinical supervision notices (`Supervised by Derek Pehle, PsyD Lic 21361`) across Hero, About section, and Footer.
- **Crisis & Emergency Disclaimers:** Visible 911 / 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline notices for immediate mental health emergencies.
- **Two-Tier Booking Flow:**
  - _Tier 1 (Public):_ Low-friction scheduling for a **Free 15-Minute Fit Consultation** via Cal.com and direct text/call.
  - _Tier 2 (Private):_ Recurring $80/session appointments reserved for established clients.
- **Visual Design:** Serene, grounding, and earth-toned aesthetic showcasing Marcella's headshot and practice spaces at 1782 Church Street in San Francisco.
- **SEO & Schema.org:** Dynamic `MedicalBusiness` / `Therapist` Schema.org JSON-LD generation with local geo-coordinates, hours, and full OpenGraph social cards.
- **TinaCMS Integration:** Full content editing workflow via `/admin` portal.
